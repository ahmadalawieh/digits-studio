### Prompt set / art-direction briefs

The following records the six image briefs and constraints used; it is a concise handoff record rather than a verbatim tool transcript.

1. **Fornaya morning table.** Photorealistic natural food photograph for a fictional contemporary Lebanese bakery website. Warm morning light on a café table; hands tearing a fresh zaatar manoushe above an oxblood ceramic plate, with a croissant and coffee nearby. Butter-yellow and flour tones, appetising texture, candid commercial editorial photography. No text, logos, watermarks or invented premises.
2. **Riwa Grove bottle.** Premium product photograph for a fictional Lebanese olive-oil identity. Dark forest-green 500 ml bottle on limestone, ivory paper label, second bottle behind, olive branch and oil dipping bowl; restrained Mediterranean setting and natural light. Exact label words: “RIWA GROVE”, “Extra virgin olive oil”, “Lebanon”, “500 ml”. No awards, certifications, harvest claims or watermark.
3. **Fornaya packaging.** Realistic bakery packaging art-direction mockup in warm café light: butter-yellow pastry box, ivory paper bag, oxblood coffee cup and napkin. Bold rounded lowercase “fornaya”; simple loaf mark with three diagonal scoring cuts. Exact tagline “Good mornings. Made slowly.” A cohesive, tactile commercial identity, believable ink and paper, no unrelated brands or watermark.
4. **Fornaya croissant.** Square photorealistic product photograph of one golden butter croissant on an ivory ceramic plate on a warm bakery counter. Flaky crisp layers, natural morning side light, oxblood and butter colour context. No text, labels, people or watermark.
5. **Fornaya coffee.** Square photorealistic café product photograph of a flat white with delicate latte art in an oxblood ceramic cup and saucer. Warm tabletop, natural morning light, inviting detail, uncluttered composition. No text, labels, people or watermark.
6. **Riwa Grove pouring ritual.** Photorealistic editorial product image of golden olive oil pouring from the neck of a dark green bottle into an ivory handmade ceramic bowl. Bread, olive twig and limestone tabletop, Mediterranean daylight, tactile restrained commercial composition. No text, certifications, labels or watermark.

## Editing and rebuilding

`concepts-content.json` contains the bilingual brand, product and campaign copy. `build_concepts.py` builds outlined SVGs and HTML using fontTools, HarfBuzz, Pillow and qrcode; `export_concepts.mjs` exports PNGs with Sharp. Fonts are supplied with their SIL OFL licences. SVG photos embed JPEG for portable renderer support. The original photographic WebPs remain the website assets.

Run locally: `python build_concepts.py`, `node export_concepts.mjs`, `python build_site.py`, then start the static server and run `node check_concepts.mjs` to test and capture the actual demo previews. Finish with `node build_public_kit.mjs` and `node validate-production.mjs`. Website source is static and deploys without the development image/font packages. Print cards and labels are concepts, not printer-certified production artwork.
