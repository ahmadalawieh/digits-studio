# Two complete self-initiated concept projects

Fornaya / فرنايا: a fictional contemporary Lebanese bakery aimed at neighbourhood regulars and busy morning customers. Oxblood #632B35, butter #F3E7CB, vivid tomato #DC5137, flour #FFFCF5, and dark ink #302325. Archivo bold display and Instrument Sans reading text; IBM Plex Sans Arabic. An oven-score symbol and bold generous type anchor a practical bilingual menu and demo pickup basket.

Riwa Grove / ريوا غروف: a fictional premium Lebanese olive-oil brand for design-conscious home cooks and gifting. Grove #263E32, limestone #D8D3C4, ivory #F2EEE3, olive-gold #BCA455 and dark green #172B22. Fraunces display, Instrument Sans text, IBM Plex Sans Arabic. An original olive/stone symbol and clear product hierarchy carry a bilingual storefront, working demo basket and editorial campaign.

Both projects include vector logos, palette, packaging photography, a brand board, three portrait posts, two stories, one landscape ad, bilingual captions, a four-week campaign plan, an email creative, a responsive working website, and EN/AR case studies. Marketing is a proposed plan, with measurement criteria instead of fabricated results. The fictional status appears on case studies, demo bars, basket review and asset notes. No real business address, client testimonial, certification, award, analytics result, payment or order processing is invented.

Case layout: [brief + key image] [identity + brand applications] [live website + interaction notes] [campaign assets + plan] [downloads + contact]. Demo layout: [small concept bar] [brand navigation] [hero] [products/menu + filters] [brand story] [campaign/email preview] [footer disclosure]. English left alignment and Arabic right alignment; natural scroll; keyboard, mobile and reduced-motion support.

Critique: the two brands must not be identical agency templates. Fornaya uses confident bread-like letterforms, oxblood panels, warm food and a lively menu. Riwa uses forest fields, generous serif type, an editorial product composition and restrained selection interactions. Main Digits Studio colors and section order remain intact; concepts sit beneath the existing real website work.
