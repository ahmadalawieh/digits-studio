# Digits Studio design plan

User approved the singular name Digits Studio and the yellow/dark reference on 22 October 2026. Scope: complete agency identity, logos, business cards, social posts and a website for branding, websites and marketing in Lebanon and worldwide.

## System before build

Signal yellow #FFDA46, Graphite #292A32, White #FFFFFF, Quiet grey #EEEDF2, supporting Lilac #B4A6ED. Graphite carries readable text. Yellow is a large signature surface, never small text on white.

Type: Archivo for assertive display and wordmark; Instrument Sans for navigation and reading; Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic. Local font files with SIL OFL licenses. Main logos use vector outlines, so fonts are not required by the recipient. Outlined display logo connects to the reference; solid small-size wordmark avoids narrow-gap legibility problems.

Original open D symbol: two nested horizontal paths with an opening on the left. One shape expands into composition, image crops and social frames. Do not claim hidden meanings beyond the letter D and an open path. Do not trace a screenshot or retain the reference's AI badge.

Website: white header; full yellow hero, large left-aligned 'Make your mark.' headline and an oversized original D symbol. Quiet white portfolio, open service rows, founder context, bilingual FAQ and a functional inquiry composer. English/Arabic are separate crawlable pages, mirrored using logical CSS. No stock team photography, fake outcomes, invented endorsements or unapproved prices.

## Critique before build

The first outline-only idea was too fragile for mobile, so add solid wordmarks and a dedicated 24px symbol. A black tech-dashboard layout would repeat the old site and overemphasize development; use a sunny yellow opening, white reading surfaces and real work. Avoid decorative counters, numbered cards, gradients, uppercase eyebrows and generic service card grids. Keep the image-led D composition as the memorable device; reserve movement for user actions and a single brief page entry.

## Delivery

SVG/transparent PNG logo family, black/white masters, favicon/avatar, six portrait social posts, three stories, LinkedIn cover, editable templates, QR, business-card front/back and print proof with bleed, A4 stationery/proposal cover, brand guide, brand board and complete ZIP. Website responsive and bilingual; public production website with real WhatsApp/email contact paths. Earlier brand explorations and website sources are preserved in the workspace archive. Production uses digitsstudio.online on Vercel. Existing projects are labelled as previous work by Ahmad, before the Digits Studio launch. Name/domain/handle clearance status recorded in strategy.md.

## Business card refinement / 22 October 2026

Centred on one optical axis, including the wordmark, founder details and enlarged 18 mm WhatsApp QR. Charcoal #292A32 front, signal yellow #FFDA46 wordmark, white #FFFFFF tagline, mist #EEEDF2 reverse, and a flat #3D3B34 enlargement of the original D. Archivo provides the name and wordmark; Instrument Sans provides the contact details. One large D is the memorable element; the fine frame and aligned yellow edge tabs provide a restrained finish. Keep the 85 x 55 mm trim and 3 mm bleed, with important artwork at least 5 mm inside trim.
